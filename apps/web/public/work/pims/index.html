PIMS

Production system — Inventory visibility and alerts that keep a real veterinary operation moving — a Go + PostgreSQL rewrite of a Google Apps Script system, 10 modules at full feature parity.

The spreadsheet that got a job.Veterinary inventory rewritten from Google Sheets to Go + PostgreSQL at full feature parity — stocktake, goods received, expiry alerts, ordering.

Why it exists

Built for Starlight Veterinary Hospital, where the inventory system before it was a Google Apps Script spreadsheet. The sheet had earned the job — it just could not work the shifts. So it got rebuilt in Go + PostgreSQL at full feature parity across 10 modules: the brief was the spreadsheet, delivered as software.

What it handles

  • Dashboard — stock and workflow summaries for the people on shift.
  • Ward purchase orders — lab, pharmacy, medical ward, surgical ward.
  • Internal indents — with approval and rejection workflows.
  • Goods receipt (GRN) — what actually arrived gets entered and checked.
  • Stock take & scan history — with optional AI-assisted image analysis.
  • Disposal & variance analysis — write-offs with reasons, differences named.
  • Expiry tracking — batches watched, with remarks.
  • New item spec requests — ask for an item properly, with approval.
  • Master item data — one place the catalog lives.
  • Admin — user management and data import.

Under the hood

  • Go + PostgreSQL, one page. A single binary serving a single-page interface. Migrations run themselves at startup; deployment ships as one binary with a systemd unit and Caddy config in the repo.
  • Stock has one owner. PIMS reads stock from Procura's SQLite items table (PROCURA_DB_PATH) and keeps no second copy to drift out of sync.
  • Workflow is three ticks. Every order carries raised / paid / received timestamps — a PO's state is literally which ticks are set. 77 of 157 are ticked received.
  • Approvals are configuration. Who may approve indents and specs is an env list (INDENT_APPROVERS, SPEC_APPROVERS, MASTER_ADMINS) — staffing changes are not code changes.
  • Proven where it matters. 17 test functions cover auth, procurement workflows, stock ownership boundaries, stock take, disposal, expiry, specs, dashboard, analysis and orders.

The honest edges

  • Modules wired, quiet. Goods receipt, disposal and the system audit log are built — and sitting at zero rows. Feature parity shipped; usage hasn't caught up. The counts say so.
  • The payment tick waits. 0 of 157 orders have payment marked. The column exists, the habit doesn't — ordering and receiving run hot while payment marking stays on paper.
  • One stock truth, two systems. Stock data has one owner: Procura. If the two ever disagree, Procura's ledger is the referee.
  • No roadmap claims. Everything above is either in the binary or in the database.
