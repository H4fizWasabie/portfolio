Practice project — A self-directed pet-food promotion concept built from supplied product material.

From product facts to a conversation people want to join.Mock campaign · personal project · not client work.

The brief, self-written

  • Audience — cat and dog owners looking for a clearer way to compare everyday nutrition options for their pets.
  • Objective — turn a broad product range into an approachable post that helps owners start a conversation about their pet's needs.

No client handed over this brief — I wrote it to practice what the pipelines needed: research, AI-assisted generation, structured checkpoints, and content management shaping a clear product-education story.

“Start with the clue” — one idea, three formats

  • 01 / Instagram post — hook. “Every pet has a clue.” Start with the question, not the product. CTA: save this for your next feeding conversation. Role: earn attention with a simple question before introducing product choices.
  • 02 / Carousel — educate. “Look for the clue.” Age. Size. Lifestyle. Concern. CTA: swipe to explore. Role: break a product range into clear, useful decision points.
  • 03 / Story — invite. “Tell us the clue.” What question do you have? CTA: reply to start. Role: turn content attention into a useful customer signal.

One message, adapted for different moments in a pet owner's feed.

The method

  • Four stages, on purpose. Research → AI-assisted generation → structured checkpoints → content management. The checkpoints review product wording, tone, audience fit, and next action before anything counts as done.
  • The strategy board says it out loud. Target: help pet owners begin a more informed nutrition conversation. Milestone: turn one product range into a clear educational content set. Next: adapt the strongest idea into a short-video script and carousel — stated as a plan, not a promise.
  • The concept shipped as three designs. Control room, Editorial, and Content studio — the same campaign argued in three visual languages.
  • Four supplied product images. Sensitive Stomach & Skin, the cat and dog range, c/d Urinary Care, and one supplied promotional artwork — the material the whole concept is built from.
