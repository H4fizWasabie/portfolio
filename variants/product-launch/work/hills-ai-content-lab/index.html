Hill’s AI Content Lab

Turn product information into a clear content story.

A self-directed nutrition-content project connecting research, AI-assisted drafts and structured editorial review.

  • Content strategy
  • AI-assisted drafting
  • Editorial review

The workflow

Original content concept; self-directed and unaffiliated. This is an authored workflow outline, not an app screenshot.

  1. Research the supplied material
  2. Shape one content idea
  3. Adapt it across formats
  4. Review wording and audience fit

Post: Every pet has a clue.

Begin with a useful question before introducing product choices.

Carousel: Look for the clue.

Organise the story around age, size, lifestyle and the owner’s concern.

Story: Tell us the clue.

Invite a question or reply instead of inventing a campaign result.

Why it exists

A product range can overwhelm its audience unless the facts are translated into useful questions and a coherent message.

What I built

I developed a pet-nutrition content concept from supplied product material. One message is adapted into a post, carousel and story, with checkpoints for wording, tone, audience fit and the next action.

How it is built

The project uses research, AI-assisted generation, structured checkpoints and content management. Its “Start with the clue” idea becomes a hook, an educational sequence and an invitation to reply.

What this page does not claim
