The workflow

Workflow outline; no publishing action is triggered. This is an authored workflow outline, not an app screenshot.

  1. Select a topic and prepare the script
  2. Produce voiceover, captions and video
  3. Verify and publish the media
  4. Record the outcome and prevent duplicates

Why it exists

Research, writing, voiceover, captions, rendering and publishing become fragile when every handover is manual or untracked.

What I built

I connected content workspaces into repeatable pipelines. The YouTube Shorts workflow selects a topic, prepares a script, creates voiceover and timed captions, renders the video, verifies the upload and records its outcome.

How it is built

Workspace runners coordinate staged outputs and ledgers. Local Kokoro speech, caption timing and FFmpeg rendering handle media production; publishing integrations verify the transferred media. Quota failures can hand the finished video back for manual posting.

What this page does not claim

This page explains the implemented pipeline without running it. No posts are published from the portfolio, no account credentials are exposed and no audience-growth claims are invented. The separate publishing jobs remain unchanged.
