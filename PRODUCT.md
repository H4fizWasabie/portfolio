# Hafiz Jamali portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating custom business applications and AI automation. Personal background, résumé and contact also support recruiters and collaborators.

## Product Purpose

Help a visitor understand Hafiz's work through real projects, inspect individual case studies and contact him about a practical software workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Owner-approved selection: Procura, PIMS, DD Drugs Register, Theoses, Yen, MAP, Hill's AI Content Lab and 89lab content automation. Homepage features Procura, Theoses and MAP. Detailed exploration belongs on individual project pages.

The approved first pass includes static project listings, verified case-study copy and safe visuals. Interactive applications are a later phase. Existing legacy routes, public homepage, services, databases and résumé files remain untouched. Code changes require an issue and feature-branch PR; neither merge nor deployment is authorised.

## Brand Commitments

Hafiz selected Product Launch B. Its existing variant design is binding; this is an extension, not a new identity exercise. Projects must not be casually labelled experimental. A personal-use project does not imply public adoption; a self-directed campaign does not imply a paid client relationship.

## Evidence on Hand

Source repositories and existing case-study pages substantiate capabilities. Existing Procura/PIMS fictional-data UI captures and explicitly staged Theoses captures have documented provenance. Four MAP screenshots edited with fictional text were explicitly approved for portfolio use on 2026-10-03. Unsupported metrics, testimonials and campaign results are prohibited. Workflow outlines must be identified as diagrams rather than app screenshots.

## Product Principles

- Explain the practical workflow before the technical implementation.
- Make the actual project and its limits legible.
- Preserve review gates and private operational records.
- Keep navigation and contact usable without JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive mobile and desktop pages, semantic headings, keyboard controls, reduced motion and readable text contrast. All fonts and runtime assets are local; no analytics or production connections.
